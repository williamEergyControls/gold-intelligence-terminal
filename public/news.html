How this feed works
  • Your YouTube channels and RSS feeds are checked every 10 minutes from the server. No browser calls, no API keys.
  • New items get a two-sentence AI summary, a sentiment dot and, when the story has a clear angle, a trade idea with what would prove it wrong.
  • When a desk has few stories from your sources, GDELT headlines fill in.
  • Admins add channels in Admin, News sources. Paste a channel link or @handle.
